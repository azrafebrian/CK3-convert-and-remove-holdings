Convert a holding in any of your controlled counties or erase a holding completely from the map.

## Installation and Use

1. Go To https://github.com/azrafebrian/CK3-convert-and-remove-holdings/releases and find the latest version, and **download**.
2. Extract the downloaded .zip file.
3. Copy or move halfsade_holdings folder and halfsade_holdings.mod into \Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\mod
4. Open the game launcher and add mod to a playset, and enable the mod. 
5. Play game with selected playset.

## About This Mod

### Features

1. Convert a selected constructed holding in your controlled county into all available holding types, such as castle, city, temple, tribal, nomadic settlement, herder settlement, and temple citadel.
2. Compacted together in a single decision, with a holding selector. Located in realm decision group.
3. Remove selected constructed holding in your controlled county and erase it from the map.
4. Removing a holding in an instant without having to reload a save.
5. Available to construct a new holding immediately after being removed.
6. Special building slot won't disappear after a holding is removed and reconstructed.
7. The decision is made to blend in with the game's look and mechanic.

### Requirements

1. Is a landed ruler
2. Converting a holding cost 250 golds.
3. Removing a holding cost 500 golds.

### Effects

1. County and holding modifiers.
2. All buildings and upgrades will be lost.
3. Clean and ready to construct holding (if removing a holding).
4. Holding get converted or removed, depending on your choice.
5. If there's a holding ruler like mayor or baron, they will be deposed and return to your court.

### Limitations

1. AI will not be able to use decision.
2. Disable remove building for any county capital holding.
3. Cooldown for 2 months, after converting or removing a holding.
4. Cooldown for 10 days, if decision is cancelled.

### Additional Info

- Available in a game that has already started.
- Untested but should be compatible with overhaul mods, but features like keeping special building slot might not work.
